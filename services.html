Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need experience to take the beginner course?

No prior experience is needed. The beginner course starts from centering the clay and builds up from there.

What should I wear to class?

Clothes you don't mind getting clay on, and no loose sleeves near the wheel. Aprons are provided.

Can I fire pieces I made elsewhere?

Members can bring in bone-dry greenware for firing; contact the studio first to confirm clay body compatibility.

How far in advance should I book a private event?

We recommend booking at least two weeks ahead, especially for weekend slots.
